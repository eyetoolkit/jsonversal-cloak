EXIF & Metadata Remover

Photos often carry hidden GPS coordinates, camera model, and timestamps. cloak re-encodes the image in your browser and drops all metadata — so nothing about where or when it was shot leaks when you share it.

How it works: the image is drawn onto an HTML canvas and re-exported. Canvas output does not carry EXIF/GPS/IPTC chunks, so the cleaned copy is metadata-free. JPEG is re-encoded (slightly lossy); PNG stays lossless but with metadata stripped.
